Hey! I'm Michelle

I'm a UX designer with 4+ years' experience leading research-driven products from first insight to launch. I love digging into problems, understanding people and shaping ideas that actually work in the real world, and I enjoy UI when I get to bring it all together.

At Slimming World, I led UX on the redesign of their public group search, from research and workshops through to usability testing and launch in September 2026. It's now live, people are finding groups far more easily, and I'm working with an international development partner to bring it into the app.

My route into design gives me a slightly different toolkit. I studied Psychology, where statistics and research methods gave me the grounding I now use every day to plan research and make sense of data. I then trained in software development and worked as a UX/UI designer and front-end developer, so I design with how things get built in mind. I also build the odd website for clients through my small company, PercyTech.

Before design, I spent 5 years in China as a senior teacher. I was part of the school's management team, looking after 23 teachers, and together we ran training and workshops and got our teachers ready to teach online during Covid. That's where I learnt to mentor, present and bring people with me.

Outside work you'll find me reading, walking my dog, at the gym, or testing far too many desserts.
