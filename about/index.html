Hey! I'm Michelle 🖖🏼

I'm a UX designer with 4+ years' experience leading research-driven products from first insight to launch. I love digging into problems, understanding people and shaping ideas that actually work in the real world, and I enjoy UI when I get to bring it all together.

At Slimming World, I led UX on the redesign of their public group search, from research and workshops through to usability testing and launch in September 2026. It's now live, people are finding groups far more easily, and I'm working with an international development partner to bring it into the app.

My route into design gives me a slightly different toolkit. I studied Psychology 🧠, so I'm fascinated by why people do what they do. I then trained in software development and worked as a UX/UI designer and front-end developer, so I design with how things get built in mind. Through my own studio, PercyTech, I've also designed and built sites like CakeSheds.com, a UK-wide finder with 380+ cake sheds.

Before design, I spent 5 years in China 🇨🇳 as a senior teacher, leading a team of 23 teachers, running training and workshops, and moving the whole school online during Covid. That's where I learnt to mentor, present and bring people with me, and I still use those skills every day.

Outside work you'll find me reading 📚, walking my dog 🌿, at the gym 💪, or testing far too many desserts 🍰.
