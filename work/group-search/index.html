Researching and designing a new group search experience for potential members

A research and design project focused on enhancing the group search experience by evaluating user needs, identifying barriers to group discovery, and validating solutions through usability testing.

01 — The goalHelping people find the right group

Redesign the group search and enrolment journey to help prospective members find the right group with confidence, while creating stronger connections between members and Consultants through improved communication and clearer information.

02 — ObjectivesResearch objectives

  • 01Improve our group search based on location.
  • 02Allow members to enrol for the group service digitally.
  • 03Set the groundwork for potential members to pay for a group subscription online.
  • 04Optimise our search engine performance to pull in more potential members.
  • 05Show potential members more before they join, so they understand what to expect in group and which Consultant might suit them best.
  • 06Mirror the web group search experience in the app.
  • 07Track potential members at every stage of the group search conversion funnel.

03 — ApproachHow I ran discovery

Before designing anything, I wanted to understand how people find a group today, what they actually do on the site, and what other brands do well. Discovery ran in four steps.

  • 01Finding a groupReviewed every route to finding a Slimming World group, noting the strengths and opportunities of each.
  • 02Data insightsWrote down our assumptions first, then tested them with Digital Insights data and Hotjar recordings.
  • 03Competitor analysisCompared location finders from different sectors and pulled out the key opportunities.
  • 04IdeationWrote user stories, then ran a workshop to sketch solutions to each one.
