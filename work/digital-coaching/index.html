01 — The briefAssessing the value of a coaching feature

The aim: understand whether introducing coaching could enhance the online member experience, and whether it's feasible.

The project involved researching competitors' digital coaching implementations, evaluating how AI contributes to user engagement and outcomes, and using these insights to inform decisions about the potential benefits and feasibility of adding such a feature.

02 — BackgroundWhat are digital and AI coaching?

Digital coaching

  • Real-time coaching: live, personal conversations between a coach and coachee via audio or video.
  • Human-led support: despite being tech-enabled, coaching is still provided by a real human.
  • Safe & honest space: a comfortable environment for open discussions on goals and challenges.

AI coaching

  • Personalised support: machine learning provides tailored guidance, feedback and support.
  • Scalable automation: mimics human coaches digitally, often as chatbots, enabling broader accessibility.
  • Adaptive learning: learns from interactions to refine advice and recommend relevant interventions.

How digital coaching can support behaviour change

  • Goal setting helps drive behaviour change by turning information into actionable steps.
  • Effective goal setting requires self-tracking to support progress and boost self-efficacy.
  • Wellness technology is more effective with adaptive or personalised goals rather than fixed ones.
  • Digital coaching impacts lifestyle factors, influencing behaviour beyond moments of direct use.

Benefits of digital and AI coaching

  • Digital coaching has increased significantly since the COVID-19 pandemic.
  • Digital coaching offers cost and time efficiency, while AI coaching enhances flexibility, cost savings and anonymity.
  • AI can adapt as a coach character based on the client's needs, and learn from data how best to react to situations.
  • An integrated approach of AI and human coaching works best, with clear indicators of which is guiding the conversation.
  • AI must evolve towards inclusivity by involving diverse people in its design and testing, ensuring cultural and social sensitivity.

Ethical risks to consider

  • 01Loss of nonverbal communication and difficulty establishing a secure, trusting relationship.
  • 02Data security mismanagement, and personal data from the client, coach and session being exposed.
  • 03Digital coaching environments can be discriminatory, designed for healthy and technically skilled people.
  • 04It can be hard to provide emotional support, or manage distress and misunderstandings, online.
  • 05AI making autonomous decisions beyond guidelines raises questions about accountability and ethics.
  • 06AI has shown gender and racial bias, driven by both the data and who makes the decisions.

03 — Data & insightsWhy members need more support

Digital support data

Support data revealed a significant volume of member queries to the digital support team around nutrition, weight loss and the plan: areas where personalised coaching could add real value. These weren't basic technical issues, but in-depth, guidance-based questions about Slimming World, the app and the wider programme.

Members are actively looking for deeper, more personalised support beyond static app content. A coaching feature could meet this need, reduce pressure on support teams and improve member experience with timely, tailored guidance.

Cancellation survey

I reviewed data from the Slimming World Online member cancellation survey, shared when members choose to leave. I focused on three support-related questions, using data from September 2024 with 495 responses.

"What do you feel has stopped you from achieving your goals?"

Key themes

  • Lack of accountability and commitment
  • Insufficient support from Slimming World Online
  • The service not feeling tailored to individual needs
  • Difficulty accessing the right resources
  • Goals that were harder to achieve than expected

Opportunity for digital coaching to…

  • Support members in setting realistic, achievable goals
  • Provide more personalised guidance
  • Introduce an early coaching touchpoint (e.g. after the first weigh-in)
  • Improve awareness and accessibility of resources

"Is there any further support you would have liked?"

Members wanted

  • More personalised support
  • Additional support when weight loss stalled
  • Help with changing mindset
  • Greater accountability
  • Better guidance to relevant resources
  • Support with setting goals

Digital coaching could…

  • Deliver personalised, realistic goal setting
  • Provide early support, e.g. a session after the first weigh-in
  • Offer targeted help for members who've maintained or gained weight
  • Support behaviour change and a positive mindset shift

"Is there anything we could have done differently so you'd have stayed?"

The most common response related to the overall user experience, including increased support, app and service improvements, renewal reminders and the option to pause membership.

The pattern

Across support enquiries and the cancellation survey, members were ultimately asking for more support. Digital coaching is one approach explored in response to that need.

04 — Competitor analysisHow others coach their users

Simple

An intermittent fasting and weight-loss app that combines habit tracking with personalised guidance from AI coach Avo: daily check-ins, nutrition scores for logged meals and tips to build healthier habits.

Daily check-in

  • Each day starts with a check-in with Coach Avo, who creates a daily plan based on the conversation.
  • The plan can be edited if it feels too challenging, or not challenging enough.
  • Goals appear on the home page with further information and resources.

AI coach

  • Daily motivational coaching that's positive, compassionate and solution-focused.
  • Actions tailored to how energised the user feels and the progress they're making.
  • Encourages self-compassion and a growth mindset, alongside meal and workout plans.

BetterMe

A health app with workouts, meal plans, trackers and self-care challenges, using fasting for weight loss and offering daily goals with progress tracking and guided workouts.

  • Articles and tips covering well-being, nutrition and fitness, plus interactive daily readings focused on mindset.
  • Users can pick a coach for an online nutrition consultation or workout, at an additional cost of £19.99 to £49.99.

Noom

Focuses on weight loss through psychology-based behaviour change, with colour-coded food logging, daily interactive lessons and personalised coaching supported by AI tools.

  • Every user can have a personal coach to support them throughout their experience.
  • Welli, Noom's AI assistant, offers 24/7 support and immediate answers to complement human coaching.
  • The vision combines human coaches for accountability and connection with AI for fast, data-driven insights.

WeightWatchers

A global programme using a points-based system to encourage balanced eating and healthy habits, supported by an app and community coaching.

  • WW Coaches help set goals and stay accountable, with filters to find the right coach (virtual workshops only).
  • Bite-size audio coaching teaches specific skills, like handling late-night snacking.
  • A 24/7 virtual assistant answers questions and points to resources.

06 — ConceptsHigh level ideas

Building on these opportunities, I developed high-level ideas showing how coaching could be integrated across different scenarios, with mid-fidelity wireframes to make each concept tangible.

Coaching as a feature of the Gold package

  • 1-1 coaching sessions as an extra benefit depending on the package, or available for all packages.
  • In the wireframes, coaching is added as an extra benefit of the Gold package.

A coaching session after the first weigh-in

Promote a 1-1 coaching session after the first weigh-in during onboarding, to encourage participation and support a successful start.

69.5% of members weigh in during week one.

Coaching for the member type

  • Identify the member's type and mode to deliver content and suggested sessions at the right stages of their journey.
  • Members feeling "OK but not at the moment" could benefit from a session to address struggles, adding to the consultant's motivational toolkit.
  • Members struggling with weight loss or maintenance could benefit from weekly sessions to identify challenges.
  • After weight maintenance or gain, offer a session through personalised support, starting with a bot or support staff for booking.

Coaching for members requesting additional support

  • Offer coaching proactively for members seeking extra support, such as preparing for events and holidays.
  • Limit to one session per month for subscribed or purchased packages, excluding extra support for members facing difficulties.
  • Make booking always available by adding it to the navigation menu.

07 — Next stepsWhere do we go from here?

This concept is an early exploration of how coaching and partnerships could enhance the member experience. As a next step, the Partnerships team at Slimming World may review the idea in more detail, considering its potential value, feasibility and alignment with wider business priorities.

That could involve exploring partner opportunities, understanding operational considerations and assessing how it fits within existing member journeys. Any progression would depend on further validation and prioritisation alongside other initiatives in the product pipeline.
